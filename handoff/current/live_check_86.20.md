Two things to read carefully rather than skim:

- **`Strong Sell` has ZERO rows in this corpus.** The sell-side gap is real in
  code and is the fail-dangerous half -- `"Strong Sell"` matches neither
  `_SELL_RECS` nor `_DOWNGRADE_RECS`, so such a position would not be exited by
  either branch and only the stop-loss could close it -- but it is LATENT today,
  not live. Only the buy side has occurrences: 5 rows, 1 genuine.
- **The step text's "higher than any row that did match" is FALSE.** `Buy` also
  matches after `.upper()` and reaches **8.80** genuine (the `maxgen` column),
  above `Strong Buy`'s 8.36. The true statement is narrower: 8.36 is higher than
  any row spelled with the literal uppercase `BUY`, and `Strong Buy` is the
  highest-scoring genuine recommendation that FAILS to reach the gate.

No lost-trade or lost-P&L claim is made: reaching the buy-candidate stage is not
trading -- Risk Judge sizing, sector caps and available cash all sit downstream.
