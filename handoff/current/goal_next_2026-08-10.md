## Traps, updated with last night's evidence

- **Derive every number LAST and generate it from a command.** Three Q/A cycles
  died on numbers invalidated by the next edit.
- **`HEAD` is not a stable anchor.** The auto-changelog hook commits on top of
  every fix, so `HEAD` and `HEAD~1` both move away from your pre-fix tree within
  minutes. Pin the SHA in any artifact that quotes a `--against-git-rev` result.
- **Splice the masterplan textually, never `json.dump`** (that once cost a
  24,200-line diff). And a Bash/python edit does **not** fire the auto-commit
  hook — it matches Write/Edit only — so verify `git log -1` in the same turn.
- **A stated limitation needs the same verification as a stated result.** I
  wrote "no NAV rendered" into a gate artifact when my capture had succeeded: an
  nb-NO **comma decimal** defeated my regex, and the em-dash I found belonged to
  a different widget. Open the artifact and look; do not conclude from a failed
  extraction.
- **A drift/agreement check cannot detect a SHARED error.** Two predicates that
  agree and are both wrong pass every agreement test. Ground-truth at least one
  of them against reality.
- **The Q/A rail drops on long prompts.** Two spawns died at 178K/179K tokens
  with no StructuredOutput call, both recovered by re-running with a lean
  prompt. An empty return is **NO VERDICT, never PASS**.
- **Watch for peers:** `away-session-pm` at 22:00 and `away-session-am` at
  07:30. Only ONE session flips steps, and `git add -A` cross-attributes — stage
  with explicit pathspecs, and keep captures inside `handoff/`, never at the
  repo root.
