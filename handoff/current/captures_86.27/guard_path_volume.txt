WHAT THIS SETTLES
-----------------
* A socket-layer audit hook costs ~1.9 us per connect and 0.10 ms across a 99-second
  run. The "blocking call inside a guard path" objection does not apply to it: the hook
  reads an already-resolved tuple and performs no name resolution of its own.
* Every non-8000 port observed (60484-60519) sits inside this machine's ephemeral range
  49152-65535 (sysctl net.inet.ip.portrange.first/last). No stub binds 8000.
* 16 of 34 urlopen calls target :8000, and each produces ~2 connect events because the
  `localhost` address list is walked (::1 then 127.0.0.1). A string-layer predicate that
  resolves would therefore fire 16 times -- but only on DISTINCT host strings once
  memoised, and only after the port gate has already excluded every other call.
* An EARLIER instrumented run over the step's own verification command
  (test_phase_86_6_subprocess_channel.py + test_phase_4000_2_cc_rail_smoke.py, 40 passed)
  recorded ZERO urlopen calls and ZERO connect events in the pytest process: all of that
  module's HTTP happens in CHILD processes, which is precisely why the subprocess channel
  needed its own guard and why a parent-process-only measurement would understate the
  surface.
